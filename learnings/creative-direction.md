# Creative direction (playful survival)

## What to aim for

The project is steering toward **survival** (zombies, threats, scavenging, pressure) while staying **goofy and readable** — TABG-adjacent personality, pill characters, silly weapons welcome.

## What to avoid

- **Grim tactical** tone, military LARP, or self-important “operator” fantasy
- **Nitty-gritty realism** as a goal: micromanagement, tedious “authenticity,” gray miseryporn
- Replacing toyetic identity with gritty humanoids or faux-documentary framing

## Corrected understanding

“Survival” here means **stakes and fantasy**, not a hardcore life sim. Funny, stylish, and mechanically clear usually beats serious and grounded; see [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).
